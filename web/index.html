TIMI Risk Score — UA/NSTEMI

The original 7-point TIMI score for suspected unstable angina or non-ST-elevation myocardial infarction. All seven criteria must be assessed. Results display historical 14-day composite event rates, not individual predictions.

Clinical criteria

Count family history of CAD, hypertension, hypercholesterolemia, diabetes, and current smoking.
